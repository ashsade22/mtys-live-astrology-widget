# More Than Your Sun Horoscope Voice Rules

These rules apply to every future Daily and Weekly horoscope set. They change editorial interpretation only. They do not change dates, signs, houses, aspects, orbs, Astronomy Engine calculations, routing, collection fields, or `sourceTransits`.

## Voice

Write in a conversational, clear, specific, grounded, human, emotionally intelligent voice that is easy to understand on the first read. Translate the astrology into a recognizable situation. Do not write like a transit summary or expose house keywords as a list of possible categories.

Clarity comes before cleverness. Not every sentence needs to be quotable.

## Set rules

1. Treat `sourceTransits` as the source of truth.
2. Preserve every major astrological factor and every protected CMS field.
3. Do not use em dashes, en dashes, hyphens, or other dash characters in horoscope prose.
4. Read all 12 entries together before approval.
5. Vary openings, sentence lengths, and paragraph rhythms across the set.
6. Do not default to opening with `A conversation`, `A plan`, `A decision`, `The real issue`, or `The practical issue`.
7. Do not rely on `The week becomes less about X and more about Y` as a standard construction.
8. The openings and contrast construction above may appear once when they are genuinely the most natural wording. They must not become templates.
9. Where several house meanings are possible, choose the clearest lived story instead of stacking categories.
10. Confirm that each entry is specific enough that moving it to another sign would make it inaccurate.

## Public structures

Daily keeps `The Mood`, `Today’s Focus`, and `Your Best Move`.

Weekly keeps `The Big Picture`, `What to Watch`, and `Your Next Move`.

Do not broaden a focused editorial pass into a rewrite of entries that already work.
